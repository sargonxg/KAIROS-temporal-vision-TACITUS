# Code Of Conduct

KAIROS follows a simple professional standard: contributors should keep discussion technical, specific, respectful, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Critique code, claims, tests, and documentation rather than people.
- Keep security-sensitive findings private until they are addressed.
- Respect that political and conflict-analysis tools can touch sensitive real-world contexts.
- Label uncertainty clearly when discussing product capability, evidence, or inference quality.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private keys, sensitive case text, or vulnerability details in public channels.
- Misrepresenting staged, mock, or experimental behavior as production-grade capability.
- Using the project to target or endanger people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in blocked participation.
